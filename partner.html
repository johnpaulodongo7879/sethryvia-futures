Our partnership principles

Shared goals.
Clear roles.
Mutual accountability.

We seek relationships that build durable local capability while supporting dependable, appropriately resourced implementation.

Local ownership

Communities and responsible government teams help shape the work from the beginning.

Defined contribution

Agree responsibilities, decision-making, budgets and expected results before delivery begins.

Responsible growth

Match commitments to technical competence, readiness and available resources.

Useful evidence

Measure whether people reach services and remain in care, and use the findings to improve.
