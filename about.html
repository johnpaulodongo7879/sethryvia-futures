About Sethryvia Futures

Local knowledge.
Shared purpose.
Stronger health systems.

We are a Ugandan non-governmental organisation and company limited by guarantee, headquartered in Wakiso District.

Our vision

Every family has the opportunity to thrive.

Communities in Uganda where every family enjoys good health, dignity and the opportunity to thrive.

Our mission

To strengthen locally led community and primary healthcare systems so that underserved people, particularly women, children, adolescents and low-income communities, obtain timely, respectful and continuous care.

Our strategic direction · 2026–2029

A focused approach to lasting change.

Community & primary care

Strengthen the skills, coordination and connections between community actors and health facilities.

Continuity of care

Reduce avoidable loss between identification, referral, diagnosis, treatment and continued care.

Health-enabling support

Integrate nutrition, WASH and health education when they address a defined barrier to better health.

Data & learning

Use practical tools, credible evidence and regular review to improve delivery.

Institutional strength

Build reliable governance, financial controls and equitable partnerships that support responsible growth.

Our commitments

Build trust into the way we work.

Our approach prioritises community participation, respectful care, inclusion, confidentiality and safeguarding. We work through existing structures and choose tools that local teams can use and sustain.

Our initial focus is Kampala and Wakiso. Wider implementation will follow demonstrated need, appropriate partnerships and adequately resourced delivery arrangements.
