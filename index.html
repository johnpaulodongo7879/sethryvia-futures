Why we exist

Care should continue beyond the first contact.

A referral is only the beginning. People need support to reach a facility, receive a diagnosis, start treatment and stay connected to care.

We focus on these practical gaps. Working through community health workers, authorised facilities and government structures, we design pathways that bring care closer and help people follow through.
