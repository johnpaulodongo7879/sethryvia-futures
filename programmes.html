Our programmes

Better connections.
Better pathways to care.

Our programme priorities bring community systems, clinical linkages and practical learning together around the people who need them most.

Technical focus
01

RMNCAH

Reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health: community engagement, early identification, supported referrals and follow-up.

02

Communicable diseases

TB, HIV and malaria: strengthen the connection between community identification, testing, treatment and retention.

03

Neglected tropical diseases

Community case-finding and referral pathways, with facility readiness and specialist collaboration informing programme design.

04

Noncommunicable diseases

Diabetes and hypertension: link early identification to assessment, counselling and continuing care.

05

Nutrition & WASH

Address nutrition, hygiene and water-related barriers where they directly improve health and service access.

06

Data & implementation learning

Simple referral tools, routine-data review and appropriately governed research to improve community health delivery.

Project development portfolio

Ideas built around real care gaps.

The initiatives below are proposals under development or submitted for consideration. They are not presented as funded programmes or completed results.

Research proposal

LINK-TB

Closed-loop referral and tracking for people with presumptive TB, designed around Kisenyi HC IV in Kampala. The approach combines assisted referral, facility notification and follow-up.

Maternal & newborn proposal

Safe Start

A community-to-facility maternal and newborn care initiative for Kitebi–Bunamwaya, designed to improve recognition of care needs, referral and follow-through.

Concept note submitted

LINK-DM

A proposed diabetes care pathway in Kampala and Wakiso, connecting community identification with facility assessment and continued support.

Consortium proposal development

Visceral leishmaniasis pathways

A proposed Karamoja initiative linking community case-finding, supported referrals, facility readiness and vector surveillance. CUAMM is the proposed lead partner; SFL’s proposed role centres on community systems.
